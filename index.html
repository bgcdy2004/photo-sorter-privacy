Privacy Policy for Photo Sorter

Effective Date: October 7, 2026

Photo Sorter is designed to help users organize photos stored on their device. This Privacy Policy explains how Photo Sorter accesses and handles information when you use the application.

Photo and Media Access

Photo Sorter requires permission to access photos and media stored on your device so that you can view, organize, keep, or delete photos using the app.

Your photos are processed locally on your device. Photo Sorter does not upload, transmit, collect, or store your personal photos on external servers.

Deleting Photos

When you choose to delete a photo, Photo Sorter uses the photo-management features provided by your device's operating system. Depending on your device, deleted photos may be moved to a trash or recently deleted folder before they are permanently removed.

Personal Information

Photo Sorter does not require users to create an account and does not directly collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.

Advertising and Third-Party Services

Photo Sorter may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising services. These services may collect certain information, such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, approximate location, app interactions, and diagnostic information, in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Photo Sorter may use Google AdMob or other advertising services to provide advertisements within the application.

Data Security

Photo Sorter is designed to minimize the collection of user information. Photos accessed through the application remain on your device unless you choose to share or otherwise transfer them using features provided by your device.

Children's Privacy

Photo Sorter is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13. The application does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
